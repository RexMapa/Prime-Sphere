Our Story

We started PrimeSphere to give growing brands the team they need, without the overhead.

Running an online store means wearing every hat at once: developer, designer, marketer and support desk. We saw great products held back simply because their owners ran out of hours in the day.

So we brought those skills together in one team. Today we help brands launch new Shopify stores, keep them running smoothly and grow them with ads, social media, SEO and email, all from one partner that knows the business inside out.
